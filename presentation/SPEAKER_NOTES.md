## 5. Volume negates luck

Expected answer: 100 errors per 100,000 decisions in this hypothetical example. Correctness is assumed to be binary and the rate constant. The numbers are illustrative arithmetic, not measured emergency-department or payment-service error rates, and errors are not identical to patients harmed. More repetitions can reveal variability but cannot correct a systematically biased test. Images: supplied presentation.

## 11. Slow is smooth and smooth is fast

A defined sequence reduces avoidable rework. For a biomedical instrument, this can mean checking the input, verifying the processing step and deciding who acts on an unusable result. Speed follows from a rehearsed process. Students should distinguish a repeatable engineering check from evidence of clinical benefit.

## 12. Slow is smooth and smooth is fast

Expected answer: define required inputs and observable acceptance checks; reject malformed output, clear stale results and make failure visible with an accountable next step. Fail closed here means invalid output never silently becomes an invented disposition. A valid parse proves only a software property. It does not establish diagnostic accuracy or care delivery. Optional transfer: apply the same reasoning to a missing sensor sample or stale calibration. ## 14. “Be so good you can’t be ignored.”

The quotation is a career maxim supplied by the presenter. Describe the experience of interviewing at medical schools and residencies, and advising capable people whose selection outcomes did not match their preparation. Do not turn selection into a verdict on a person’s worth or promise that merit guarantees attention. The practical move is to produce useful work and make its quality legible.

## 15. Be so good you can’t be ignored

Expected answer: one bounded artifact with a source, a test that can fail, an observed result and an honest limitation. A data analysis, test procedure or human-factors study can count without writing code. State the student’s own contribution and any AI assistance. Example: deliberately inject a stale sensor value and show the procedure catches it. One clear piece of work can support a mentor or interview conversation. These proposed outcomes connect to public course descriptions on experimental design, verification, scientific communication and professional development.
