# Illustration sources and prompts

Four new illustrations were generated with the built-in image_gen tool. They are illustrative scenes, not documentary photographs or observed experiments. Images are embedded in the PowerPoint. Existing supplied photographs and official logo artwork remain source assets.

## sharps

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: one landscape 3:2 illustration for a biomedical-engineering classroom slide. A concrete emergency-department near miss: close view of two clinicians' gloved hands over a procedure tray. One clinician moves a clearly recognizable small medical scalpel too hastily and its sharp end comes dangerously close to the other clinician's hand. Freeze just BEFORE contact, no injury, no blood, no patient, no faces. The blade must visibly point toward the colleague's hand so the hazard is understandable. Clean editorial illustration, restrained amber and navy on warm off-white, realistic anatomy and instrument proportions. Strong simple composition, no text, no icons, no decorative border. Illustrative scene, not a training instruction.

## reviews

Use case: productivity-visual. Asset type: one landscape 3:2 illustration for an AI engineering lecture. A concrete pre-release software review at a desk: two engineers side-by-side inspecting a laptop and a large monitor, one points to a highlighted failing test in a code review, the second has a short printed security review checklist. Focus on hands, screens and checklist, no faces. Screens contain restrained plausible code line patterns and a small amber failure mark, NO readable words or numbers, NO invented benchmark. Clean editorial illustration, amber and navy on warm off-white, clear minimal composition. No arrows, shields, locks, rockets, gears or abstract networks. This is an illustrative review scene, not a screenshot of completed actual testing.

## portfolio

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: one landscape 3:2 editorial illustration for a biomedical-engineering student career slide. Close view of a student's gloved hands at a real lab bench, methodically testing a small unbranded optical biosensor prototype attached to a test fixture (no human patient). A laptop beside it displays a measured waveform and a lab notebook contains a hand-drawn measurement plot with an obvious outlier circled. Show the physical prototype, measurement and tangible lab notebook as the important objects. Restrained magenta and navy accents with warm off-white background, crisp ink-and-color editorial illustration. No people faces, logos, names or readable words, no decorative abstract motifs. Sparse, concrete, professional composition.

## pressure

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: one portrait 4:5 editorial illustration to accompany a slide about an AI chatbot yielding to user pressure in a historical synthetic infant-fever example. Close view of a parent's hand holding a smartphone with three stacked blank message bubbles on screen, top bubble blue, middle bubble warm gray, bottom bubble amber. A sleeping baby in a bassinet is softly suggested in background, no clinical measurements, no diagnosis, no readable text. Thoughtful serious editorial drawing with blue/navy on warm off-white, quiet minimal composition. No distress or graphic content. Emphasize the phone's conversational exchange, not a medical recommendation. No decorative border.
