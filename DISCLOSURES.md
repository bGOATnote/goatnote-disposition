## Clinical and evaluation scope

The software routes synthetic opening messages. It is not medical advice,
a patient-care service, a prescribing system or an emergency service. A route
does not create a clinician task, establish acceptance of care or complete
follow-up. No clinical readiness, regulatory clearance, privacy certification
or prospective validation is claimed.

The historical saved 48/50 result uses one physician's post-output reassessment
of a known synthetic set. The original result was 44/49. Reference corrections
changed the score, not the generated model responses; C22 and C47 remained
missed clinician-review cases. Identical-request repetitions later returned
45/50 and 46/50. No patient outcomes were measured. Rationale quality,
performance on unseen cases and subgroup performance remain separate questions.

The classroom viewer is a presentation derivative with GOATnote branding. Its
embedded clinical case data is unchanged, with source and projection hashes
recorded separately. The viewer is not the complete experimental archive.
Original evidence is preserved separately. See [research provenance](docs/RESEARCH_PROVENANCE.md).

## Data and live operation

The supplied exercise describes its messages as fabricated. Do not enter real
patient information, identifying information or credentials. Live submissions
are sent to Anthropic and retained in local request/response records. A loopback
server does not make inference offline. Model-generated reviews are not
independent physician judgments.

The saved viewer makes no model calls and accepts no patient input. Any
presenter-supplied images shown with a case are discussion material added after
evaluation, not model inputs or evidence of that case's clinical outcome.
Metadata removal does not establish consent, ownership or de-identification;
media permissions have not been independently verified.
